# EV Market & Business Analytics

##  Project Overview

EV Market & Business Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding the growth, sales performance, manufacturer landscape, vehicle-category distribution, and charging infrastructure of the Indian Electric Vehicle (EV) market.

The project transforms raw EV datasets into actionable business insights using Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI.

The goal is to demonstrate how business data can be cleaned, analyzed, queried, visualized, and converted into insights that can support business decision-making.

---

##  Problem Statement

The Indian EV market has experienced significant growth in recent years, but understanding the market requires analyzing multiple dimensions such as:

- EV sales over time
- Manufacturer performance
- Market share
- Vehicle categories
- Year-over-year growth
- State-wise EV infrastructure
- Availability of operational charging points
- Geographic concentration of EV manufacturers

The objective of this project is to build a structured analytics workflow that answers these business questions and presents the results through interactive dashboards.

---

##  Business Questions

This project focuses on answering the following questions:

1. How have EV sales changed from 2015 to 2024?
2. Which EV manufacturers have the highest overall sales?
3. Which manufacturers have the largest market share?
4. Which manufacturers experienced the highest growth?
5. Which vehicle categories contribute most to EV sales?
6. Which states have the highest number of operational charging points?
7. Which states have the highest concentration of EV manufacturers?
8. Where are EV sales and charging infrastructure concentrated?
9. What patterns can help identify potential EV market opportunities?

---

##  Dataset

The project uses publicly available Indian EV market datasets covering sales, manufacturers, vehicle categories, and charging infrastructure.

### Dataset Files

| Dataset | Description |
|---|---|
| `EV Maker by Place.csv` | EV manufacturers and their locations |
| `ev_cat_01-24.csv` | EV registrations by vehicle category and date |
| `ev_sales_by_makers_and_cat_15-24.csv` | EV sales by manufacturer and category from 2015–2024 |
| `OperationalPC.csv` | Number of operational public charging points by state |
| `Vehicle Class - All.csv` | Vehicle-class registration data |

### Dataset Coverage

- Sales period: **2015–2024**
- Registration/category data: **2015–2024**
- Geographic analysis: **Indian states**
- Manufacturer-level analysis included
